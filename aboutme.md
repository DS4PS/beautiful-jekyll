**Hello there!**

My name is Melissa Garcia and I am a current graduate student studying **Program Evaluation and Data Analytics** online at Arizona State University. I came interested in joining this program through my profession of working with a non-profit for almost five years. We partnered with another outside organization of researchers across the United States and through my role built a close connection with some of the researchers; even one of them wrote my recommendation letter! I was intrigued to work behind the scenes and evaluate the progress of a program and get insight of how effective grants can be in local communities. I am almost to the finish line and anticipate to graduate in May 2027!
